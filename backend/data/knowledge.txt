Company Name: TechNova

Internship Program

TechNova offers a six-month software development internship.

Interns work on real-world software projects involving frontend development, backend development, databases, and APIs.

Internship Duration

The standard internship duration is six months.

Working Hours

Interns generally work from Monday to Friday.

Leave Policy

Interns receive reasonable leave according to company policy.

Remote Work

Some internship positions may allow remote work depending on the team and project requirements.

Application Process

Candidates submit their resume and complete a technical interview before receiving an internship offer.

Stipend

The internship stipend depends on the role and the candidate's experience.
